Openings and closures

    This is a working sample of about 115 mills assembled from public web sources — company sites, trade press, and news coverage — not an official census and not a complete list. Mills that are small, long-closed, or simply under-covered online are likely missing entirely. Closures from 2023 to 2025 were checked against company announcements and trade press; most opening years and older closure years are approximate and should be verified before you cite them. Pre-1950 mills that closed early are heavily under-represented. To use authoritative data, load a CSV with columns name, company, city, state, lat, lon, open, close (leave close blank for operating mills).
