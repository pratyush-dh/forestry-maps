      Read before using

      Road time uses the major-road network from the Forest to Mill map, with off-road legs at 40 km/h (a hex may also go straight across country when that is faster), and has no 120-minute limit here, so it can differ from the Forest to Mill allocation. Colour is the wood removed from forests inside the polygon, averaged over all of its land, so it is not the density at any one place. The mills are a sample of open mills, so large western polygons show missing mills as much as empty forest. Removals are FIA county totals shared evenly among forest hexes of 842 km² and include land clearing, so road-time edges are accurate to about one hex and are smoothed over about 20 km for display; two mills (Fernandina Beach containerboard and Woodland) sit just outside their own road-time polygon for that reason. Two Camas, Washington, mills share one point and one polygon.
