Openings and closures

    Loaded from data/mills.csv. About 196 mills assembled from public web sources, not an official census: mostly pulp/paper mills plus a smaller sample of sawmills, plywood, OSB and MDF/particleboard mills, with many small, long-closed or under-covered mills missing. Most opening years and older closures are approximate. Per-mill production capacity is public for very few facilities (most published figures are company-wide totals, not single-mill), so capacity is filled in only where a specific figure was reported and is otherwise blank. Edit the CSV or load another one with columns name, company, city, state, lat, lon, open, close (blank close = operating; optional type, capacity, capacity_unit, checked, note).
